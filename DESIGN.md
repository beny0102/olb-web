## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, prestigious digital presence for an elite baseball federation. The design language marries the stately institutional gravitas of Olympic governance and European football leagues (such as the Premier League) with the timeless, visceral craft of heritage baseball.

Key characteristics:
- **Atmosphere & Persona:** Regal, institutional, resolute, and immaculate. The system balances sovereign administrative weight with athletic agility.
- **Target Audience:** Federation executives, franchise owners, scouts, sports journalists, and devoted supporters seeking official standings, judicial rulings, and performance telemetry.
- **Visual Stance:** Structured Architectural Modernism. The interface relies on crisp structural borders, rich contrast, bespoke athletic badges, and restrained metallic crest accents rather than frivolous gradients, drop shadows, or generic stadium neon.

## Colors

The palette leverages contrast between clean field chalk surfaces and imposing institutional pigments:

- **Surface & Ground:** Pure chalk white (`#FFFFFF`) and field off-white (`#F8F9FA`). High-priority background groupings leverage `#F1F3F5` for distinct regional framing.
- **Primary Sovereign Navy (`#071228`):** Anchors headers, primary actions, heavy badges, and major typography. Communicates supreme administrative authority.
- **Competition Crimson (`#D92534`):** Reserved for live status indicators, competition alerts, crucial timeline ticks, and primary callouts. Used with razor precision to maintain its impact.
- **Metallic Crest Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Utilized exclusively for championship signifiers, trophy seals, official league crest details, and top-tier standing indicators.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Fine-milled slate rules (`#E2E6EC` and `#CBD2DC`) that echo architectural blueprints and historical rulebooks.

## Typography

The type system blends dynamic, condensed athletic force with Scandinavian editorial neutrality:

- **Headlines & Metric Display (`Barlow Condensed`):** Set in bold and heavy weights. Headlines frequently employ uppercase styling to mimic historic stadium scoreboards and official federation bulletins. Tabular numerical stats inherit this condensed format for maximum density and high-impact readability.
- **Editorial & Institutional Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Clean, objective, modern sans-serif optimized for long-form disciplinary hearings, operational documentation, and deep data grids.
- **Labels & Badges:** `label-caps` must strictly be transformed to uppercase with elevated letter-spacing (`0.08em`) to enforce official federation nomenclature.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on a rigid 12-column architectural grid with tight horizontal tolerance and decisive vertical pacing:

- **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, `2.5rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum canvas width is capped at `1440px` for consistent balance between editorial reading width and scoreboard data columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Split tables convert to fixed horizontal scroll panes with pinned identifier columns.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` margins, `0.75rem` gutters. Dense multi-column telemetry tables convert to card stacks or condensed row summaries.
- **Pacing Rhythm:** Structural containers use strict multiples of `8px`. Section transitions rely on solid dividing rules rather than excessive blank vertical air, conveying an organized, structured ledger.

## Elevation & Depth

To break away from generic SaaS soft shadows and neon sports styling, this design system establishes depth through precision structural linework and tonal layering:

- **Planes & Outlines:** Surfaces are separated through 1px solid low-contrast rules (`#E2E6EC`) and 2px high-contrast structural seams (`#071228`).
- **Shadow Profiles:** Ambient shadows are strictly omitted for static elements. When an element requires lift (such as official flyouts, modal communiqués, or active dropdowns), use a crisp, zero-blur athletic offset:
  - *Standard Overlay:* `0px 4px 0px rgba(7, 18, 40, 0.08), 0px 1px 3px rgba(7, 18, 40, 0.05)`
  - *Active Lift / Drag:* `0px 6px 0px #071228`
- **Surface Layering:** 
  - `Base`: Chalk Canvas (`#F8F9FA`)
  - `Card / Ledger Container`: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) framed by `#E2E6EC`
  - `Admin Banner / Command Strip`: Sovereign Navy (`#071228`) with metallic gold badge accents

## Shapes

The design system enforces a sharp geometric shape language (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Corners:** Components, cards, tables, scoreboards, and buttons feature crisp `0px` corners. This references the classic masonry and architecture of historic ballparks, athletic rulebooks, and institutional governance seals.
- **Angled Accents:** Select brand elements, scoreboard caps, and status ribbons may incorporate a sharp 45-degree chamfered corner (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to denote active competition status without resorting to rounded pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Sovereign Navy background (`#071228`), text in White, uppercase `Hanken Grotesk` (`12px`, letter spacing `0.08em`), 0px border radius, 12px 24px padding. Hover: subtle lift with Competition Crimson bottom border (`2px solid #D92534`).
- **Secondary / Regulatory:** Transparent ground with a 1px solid Sovereign Navy outline. Hover: Sovereign Navy fill with white text.
- **Competition Action:** Competition Crimson (`#D92534`) background with chalk white text, used for live game entry, judicial alerts, and instant replay reviews.

### Chips & Badges
- **Championship / Honors:** Sovereign Navy fill with 1px Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`) border and Gold text.
- **Live Match Status:** Competition Crimson background, uppercase condensed lettering, accompanied by an instantaneous pulsed crimson square indicator.
- **Roster & Position:** Pure white background, 1px `#CBD2DC` border, dark navy monospaced text.

### Data Tables & Standings
- **Header:** Off-white (`#F1F3F5`) with a 2px solid Sovereign Navy baseline. Labels set in `label-caps`.
- **Rows:** Alternating rows (`#FFFFFF` and `#FAFBFC`) separated by 1px hairline rules (`#E2E6EC`).
- **Data Points:** Team records, OPS, ERA, and points set in `tabular-stats` (`Barlow Condensed`) with tabular figures alignment.

### Cards & Scoreboards
- Framed by a 1px solid slate border (`#CBD2DC`). Top banner contains a 4px accent line: Navy for general fixtures, Crimson for active live matches, and Gold for tournament finals.
- Matchup display features opposing team crests anchored by heavy condensed numerical scores.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Crisp rectangular inputs with chalk white fill, 1px `#CBD2DC` borders, and 12px padding. Focus state switches to a 2px `#071228` border with zero border-radius shift. No fuzzy focus halos.
